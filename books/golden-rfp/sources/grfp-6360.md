Introduction
The Alcatel-Lucent OmniSwitch® 6360 Stackable Gigabit Ethernet LAN Switch Family is an industry leading, branch, campus workgroup, and enterprise, value access solution. These are simple, flexible, and secure switches ideal for out-of-the-wiring-closet workstation, accesspoint, IP telephony and critical Internet of Things (IoT) deployment.
