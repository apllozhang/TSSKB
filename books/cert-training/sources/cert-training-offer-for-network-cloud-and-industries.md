CERTIFICATION
• Gaining a Certification level guarantees a minimum level of skills for the Engineer in a product.
• The certification achievement date is the date of the exam. The achieved certificate is valid for
two years by default (except where otherwise specified).
CERTIFICATION RENEWAL
Two options are available to renew a certification:
• By retaking the certification exam 
• By attending the update course when available, the recertification is granted when all the course 
modules have been completed. The certification renewal is then automatic only if the user 
satisfies one of the two following conditions:
• User has a valid (non-expired) certification
• User has an expired certification less than 2 years from the completion date of the update course.
Users who do not fulfill these conditions have to retake the certification exam.

Aruba resell
From February 1st 2025, Business Partners and end customers interested in getting trained and certified
on the HPE Aruba networking portfolio (WLAN [campus access mobility], ClearPass [network security]
and Airwave [network management]) will be redirected to HPE Aruba Education Services or HPE Aruba
Authorized Training Centers (ATC).
The advised training/certification tracks to take from HPE Aruba Training Services are the following:
• HPE Aruba Networking Certified Professional - ACP Campus Access
• HPE Aruba Networking Certified Expert - ACX Campus Access Mobility
• HPE Aruba Networking Certified Professional - ACP Network Security (ClearPass)
• HPE Aruba Networking Certified Expert - ACX Network Security (ClearPass)
• Managing Campus Networks with the Airwave Management Platform
The HPE Aruba certifications listed above taken from HPE Aruba after February 1st 2025, will be recognized 
by ALE for Global Welcome Center filtering and routing of tickets for support access.
